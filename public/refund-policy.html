Refund Policy

Last updated: [DATE].

If an event is cancelled

All paid orders for the event are refunded automatically to the original payment method. Refunds are completed once our payment provider confirms them, which can take several business days.

Other refund requests

Each organizer sets a refund policy and a last day for refund requests on their event page. You can request a refund from your order page before that deadline; the organizer reviews the request. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Postponed or rescheduled events

Tickets stay valid for the new date. Organizers may offer refunds at their discretion.
